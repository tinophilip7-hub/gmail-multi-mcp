gmail-multi-mcp

gmail-multi-mcp is a personal, self-hosted tool that lets the owner use Claude (an AI assistant) with their own Gmail and Google Workspace mailboxes. It runs only on the owner's own computer.

It is not a public service and is not offered to other users. There is no sign-up, and the app's Google OAuth client is used only by its owner to connect their own accounts.

What it does: search and read email, manage drafts, send and reply, and organise mail with labels, archive, spam and trash, at the owner's request.
